# Intentional deviations

- Hero video bandwidth is intentionally prioritized over aggressive optimization for this portfolio/concept project. Do not treat the current video weight or preload strategy as a priority design issue.

- The desktop hero CTA is intentionally full-width within the copy column. Do not flag its width as excessive unless it causes an actual responsive or usability problem.

- Luma is a fictional concept brand. Marketing claims such as "Sustainable by nature" and "Delivered & Installed", as well as testimonial names, portraits, ratings, and review content, are intentionally fictional parts of the concept. Do not require real-world evidence or flag them as deceptive/unverified proof during critique. Their visual treatment, specificity, copy quality, and UX may still be critiqued.

- Different hero media and layout breakpoints are intentional. Do not flag the 768–1023px range solely because the media and composition breakpoints differ; only flag it if visual inspection shows an actual crop, focal-point, or readability problem.

- The hero sequence intentionally begins with cabin footage and introduces lifestyle/amenity footage later. Do not assume the hot-tub footage is the opening product impression.

- The 10px uppercase tracked mobile proof line is an intentional compact metadata treatment. Do not flag its font size alone unless visual testing shows an actual readability or accessibility problem.

- The Models Overview is intentionally a cinematic visual browsing section rather than a dense model comparison/selection interface. Image-led thumbnails are intentional; detailed model comparison and specifications belong to the following Models Showcase. Do not flag image-only thumbnails or the lack of persistent labels alone as a discoverability issue unless users cannot determine the active model.

- Model order does not need to match across the Models Overview and Models Showcase. The overview order is chosen for visual pacing and imagery, with Veyra intentionally first because its brighter image creates a stronger section opening. Do not flag differing model order alone as a consistency issue.

- Models Overview controls are intentionally visually restrained. Desktop previous/next arrows appear on hover/focus, while mobile prioritizes swipe/drag and visible thumbnails. Do not flag hidden-at-rest navigation controls alone as a discoverability problem.

- The compact 40px play/pause control is intentional within this composition. Do not flag its size alone unless testing reveals an actual interaction/accessibility problem.

- The 4-second autoplay and subtle progress treatment are intentional parts of the cinematic carousel experience.

- The testimonial popover intentionally mounts with visibility: hidden for a few animation frames while its expanded height and viewport position are measured, then becomes visible before the shared-frame morph begins. Do not flag this preparation state as a broken or invisible popover unless the interaction actually fails to complete in visual testing.

- The testimonials carousel intentionally uses slow ambient autoplay with direct drag navigation on both desktop and mobile rather than persistent arrows, pagination, or pause controls. Autoplay pauses during hover, focus, pointer interaction, and expanded testimonial viewing. Do not flag the lack of persistent carousel controls alone unless users cannot directly reach or read testimonials.

The draggable divider and handle are optional enhancement cues; users do not need to discover or manipulate the slider to understand the section. Do not flag the lack of explicit drag instructions alone unless the static presentation becomes unclear or misleading.

- Interior Comparison labels are intentionally concise and editorial. `Focus / Light oak` and `Unwind / Dark walnut` complement the visible workspace and lounge imagery rather than restating it in functional terms. More descriptive image alt text is intentional for accessibility. Do not flag the labels for lacking workspace/lounge descriptors alone unless the visual distinction is genuinely unclear.

- The Interior Comparison intentionally opens at a 56/44 split for visual composition and image framing rather than to imply a preference between `Focus` and `Unwind`. Both directions are presented as equal examples. Do not flag the non-50/50 starting position alone unless it creates an actual visual or semantic imbalance.
