# Luma Cabins — Landing Page Brief

## Project Overview

Build a premium homepage-style landing page for a fictional modular cabin brand.

The project is intended as an Upwork portfolio piece that demonstrates the ability to take a premium brand idea from strategy to structure, visual direction, responsive frontend implementation, and deployment.

## Stack

- Next.js
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- Vercel

## Content Approach

- Hardcoded typed content
- No CMS
- Content should be structured so it can be reused across components
- Exact copy is not locked yet
- The page should support multiple visual/design variants before final direction is chosen

---

## Project Goal

The goal is to show that I can design, develop, and ship a polished premium landing page.

This portfolio piece should appeal to clients who need high-quality landing pages for premium visual or service-based brands, including:

- Architecture studios
- Interior design studios
- Real estate brands
- Wellness brands
- Hospitality businesses
- Creative studios
- High-end local service businesses

The project should feel like a complete premium landing page delivery, not just a UI experiment.

## Brand

## Name

**Luma Cabins**

## Category

Premium modular cabins.

## Region Feel

European premium cabin brand, not tied too strongly to one specific country.

The brand can feel suitable for forests, lakes, mountains, and rural plots across Europe.

Avoid overly specific permit, pricing, or delivery promises tied to one legal region.

## Tone

The tone should feel:

- Calm
- Minimal
- Premium
- Nature-focused
- Warm
- Quietly confident
- Architectural but not cold
- Sustainable but not preachy

The brand should feel refined and human, not corporate or over-technical.

## Anti-goals

The page should not feel like:

- Cheap tiny-house content
- Survivalist or prepper off-grid housing
- Generic real estate development
- A cold corporate architecture firm
- A fake award-winning studio
- A tech-startup SaaS website
- Mass-market prefab housing
- Overly rustic cabin branding

## Audience

## Primary Audience

Design-conscious city professionals, couples, or small families who want a compact premium weekend retreat in nature.

They are likely interested in:

- Escaping city life
- Spending weekends in nature
- Owning a calm second place
- Avoiding the complexity of a fully custom build
- Getting a beautiful, low-maintenance cabin

## Secondary Audience

People interested in simpler living, remote work, slower living, or optional off-grid capability.

This can include people who want a compact home for a more intentional lifestyle, but the brand should not feel like survivalist off-grid housing.

## Audience Values

The audience cares about:

- Premium design
- Nature
- Simplicity
- Sustainability
- Low maintenance
- Clear process
- Quality materials
- Energy performance
- A calmer lifestyle
- Avoiding complicated custom construction

## Offer

Luma Cabins offers pre-designed modular cabins with curated customization and optional add-on modules.

The offer is closer to a premium modular cabin product than a traditional architecture agency.

## Core Offer

Choose a cabin model, customize selected finishes and systems, add optional modules, prepare the site, then install.

## What It Is

- Pre-designed cabin models
- Curated customization
- Modular add-ons
- High-performance compact homes
- Premium retreat spaces
- Solar-ready by default
- Optional off-grid packages

## What It Is Not

- Fully custom architecture
- Cheap tiny homes
- Survival cabins
- A generic prefab catalog
- A mass-market housing product

## Add-on Modules

Supported modules:

- Sauna module
- Workspace module
- Storage / utility module
- Deck / terrace extension

## Customization

Customization should feel curated, not unlimited.

## Principle

The core architecture stays fixed. Clients customize finishes, energy setup, and selected add-on modules.

This supports the brand promise of a faster, simpler, higher-quality modular cabin process without the complexity of a fully custom build.

## Possible Customization Options

Exterior finish:

- Timber
- Charred wood
- Standing seam metal

Interior palette:

- Light oak
- Warm ash
- Darker walnut-style finish

Energy setup:

- Grid-ready
- Solar-ready
- Optional off-grid package

Modules:

- Sauna
- Workspace
- Storage / utility
- Deck / terrace

## Sustainability and Performance

Sustainability claims should be credible and specific.

Use claims like:

- Compact footprint
- High-performance insulation
- Responsibly sourced timber
- Low-waste prefabrication
- Solar-ready energy setup
- Durable, repairable material choices

Avoid overclaims like:

- Carbon neutral
- Zero impact
- 100% sustainable
- Fully off-grid by default

## Off-grid Positioning

Solar-ready by default, off-grid optional.

The cabins are designed for efficient everyday comfort, with optional packages for remote plots, solar energy, water storage, and greater independence.

The page should not feel like survivalist or prepper off-grid housing.

## Homepage Section Skeleton

## 1. Hero

Purpose:

Sell the feeling first.

The hero should communicate:

- Modular cabins
- Slower living
- Nature retreat
- Compact high-performance homes
- Sustainable footprint

## Hero Media

The hero should use cinematic video footage showing cabins in nature.

Video direction:

- Slow camera movement
- Morning or evening light
- Exterior cabin shots
- Forest, lake, or mountain surroundings
- Maybe a few interior glimpses
- Calm, premium, atmospheric feeling

## Hero Content

Include:

- Short premium headline
- Short supporting text
- Primary CTA
- Secondary CTA

Exact headline, subheadline, and CTA text are not locked yet.

---

## 2. Cabin Models

Purpose:

Show the product range quickly.

Must include:

- Niva
- Aster
- Veyra
- Basic specs
- Short use-case description
- Path to view model/details

No pricing on the homepage for now.

Pricing can be explored later on detail pages or a separate pricing page.

---

## 3. Modular System / Customization

Purpose:

Explain that the cabins are pre-designed but configurable.

Must communicate:

- Fixed core architecture
- Curated customization
- Optional modules
- Faster and simpler than custom building
- Quality and performance benefits of a controlled system

Possible content themes:

- Choose a model
- Add only what you need
- Customize finishes and energy setup
- Keep the build process predictable

---

## 4. Why Modular Cabins

Purpose:

Explain the value proposition.

Possible points:

- Faster path from idea to installation
- Lower waste prefab production
- Compact footprint
- Designed for remote and nature plots
- Predictable system
- Premium materials
- Better energy performance
- Simpler decision-making than a fully custom build

---

## 5. Portfolio / Selected Cabin Concepts

Purpose:

Add architectural credibility and visual richness.

Possible section names:

- Selected cabins
- Cabins in context
- Built for different landscapes
- Retreat concepts

Possible fictional concept examples:

- Forest retreat
- Lakeside cabin
- Hilltop workspace cabin

The section can show fictional concept examples without pretending they are real client projects.

In the portfolio/Upwork case study, the project can be described as a concept project.

The landing page itself does not need to label itself as fictional.

---

## 6. Performance & Sustainability

Purpose:

Build trust with practical proof.

Include:

- High-performance insulation
- Responsibly sourced timber
- Solar-ready setup
- Low-waste prefabrication
- Durable materials
- Optional off-grid package
- Compact footprint

Keep this section visual and concise.

Avoid making unsupported sustainability claims.

---

## 7. How It Works

Purpose:

Reduce uncertainty and explain the buying/building process.

Suggested steps:

```txt
Choose your model
Customize finishes and modules
Review your site
Prepare foundations and utilities
Produce the cabin
Deliver and install
```

This section should make the process feel simpler and less intimidating than a custom architecture/build project.

---

## 8. FAQ

Purpose:

Answer practical concerns and build trust.

Suggested FAQ topics:

- Do I need to own land before contacting you?
- Can the cabins be installed off-grid?
- How customizable are the models?
- What foundation is required?
- How long does production and installation take?
- Are permits included?
- Can I add modules later?
- Where do you deliver?

The FAQ should be practical, not overly brand-focused.

---

## 9. Final CTA

Purpose:

Convert serious visitors.

CTA intent:

- Explore models
- Book a consultation

The final CTA should feel calm and premium, not pushy.

- Image-led
- Premium
- Calm
- Spacious
- Clear
- Responsive
- Easy to scan
- Practical after the emotional hero
